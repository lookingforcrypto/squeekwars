Deploy Season 2

This page deploys the five Squeek Wars Season 2 contracts from your own wallet: GameConfig, SeedLedger, Clans, Army and Villages, in that order. Your wallet becomes the owner of the rule table. Each contract is one transaction you confirm; gas is paid in Sepolia ETH. Nothing else is sent anywhere.
