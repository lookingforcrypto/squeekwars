How it works

One village per Squeek. Build it up, let the hamster boost it, and raid neighbours who are at least twice as weak.

Build

Town Hall, Walls, 8 production sites and 12 plots for Barracks, Academy, Storehouses, Vaults and more.

Grow with the wheel

Every night Squeektoshi runs. His laps pay SEED to every village and boost Wood, Food, Stone and Iron output.

Fight

Train six unit types and raid on a 100 × 100 world map. Win only with twice the defender's power.

Lead with your Squeek

Your NFT is your champion. It levels up from battles and from the hamster's runs, up to level 30.

Rules

The game rules are enforced by the contracts. The fair-play rules are ours: villages that break them can be hidden from the site's leaderboards and season rewards.

How the game works

  1. One village per Squeek NFT. The village and its champion follow the NFT when it changes hands.
  2. Raids take resources only: Wood, Food, Stone and Iron. SEED, NFTs and buildings are never taken.
  3. You win only with twice the defense. Then every defender falls and you can loot; otherwise both sides lose troops and nothing is taken.
  4. New villages get a 7-day shield. They cannot attack or be attacked.
  5. A village can be raided successfully at most 3 times a day. What sits in its Vault is safe.
  6. No trading between players. The Market only exchanges your own resources, and SEED cannot be exchanged.

Fair play

  1. No feeder accounts. Don't run extra wallets just to be raided by, or to feed, a main village.
  2. No bug abuse. If something gives you resources or wins it shouldn't, report it instead of using it.
  3. Keep names clean. No offensive, hateful or impersonating player or clan names.
  4. Leave the site alone. Don't attack or overload the website, the contracts' readers or other players' devices.
  5. Report, don't retaliate. Use the report form below for bugs and rule-breakers.

FAQ

Where does it run?

Season 1 runs on the Sepolia testnet, next to Squeekchain's own contracts.

Can someone take my Squeek?

No. Raids only take Wood, Food, Stone and Iron. SEED, NFTs and buildings are never taken.

Do I need to be online to defend?

No. Battles resolve on arrival with the troops and Walls you have at home.

Can I trade with other players?

No player trading. The Market only exchanges your own resources, which keeps multi-accounts from feeding a main village.
