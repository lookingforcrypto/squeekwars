Play on testnet

Build a hamster village. Grow with a real wheel.

Squeek Wars is a strategy game on Squeekchain. Every wallet can found one village, and Squeektoshi's nightly runs pay SEED and boost production for everyone. Holding Squeeks adds a champion and faster building.

The live game reads your wallet and the Squeeks in it. This demo has no wallet or chain: you play a wallet holding three Squeeks, and time runs 60× faster.

How it works

One village per wallet. Build it up, let the hamster boost it, and raid neighbours who are at least twice as weak.

Build

Town Hall, Walls, 8 production sites and 12 plots for Barracks, Academy, Storehouses, Vaults and more.

Grow with the wheel

Every night Squeektoshi runs. His laps pay SEED to every village and boost Wood, Food, Stone and Iron output.

Fight

Train seven unit types, scout and raid on a 100 × 100 world map. Win only with twice the defender's power.

Bring your Squeeks

Holding Squeeks makes construction faster after good runs, and one of them becomes your champion, levelling up to 30. Its level stays with the Squeek.

The wheel bonus

Output per hour = base × (1 + wheel bonus)
Wheel bonus = last night's laps ÷ 4,320
capped at +25% per Wheelhouse level
Last recorded night: 11,182 laps → +259% before the cap. A village with a level 6 Wheelhouse gets +150%. Every village also receives 1,118 SEED from the same run. If the hamster rests, base output keeps going.

Units

Attack and defense power decide every battle. There is no luck: the battle calculator shows the result before you send. Scouts find weak spots for +10% attack on a village for 12 hours.

Rules

The game rules are enforced by the contracts. The fair-play rules are ours: villages that break them can be hidden from the site's leaderboards and season rewards.

How the game works

  1. One village per wallet, open to everyone. Production, SEED and the wheel bonus are the same with or without a Squeek. Holding Squeeks gives one champion and faster construction after good runs (up to −30%, each extra Squeek adding less).
  2. Raids take resources only: Wood, Food, Stone and Iron. SEED, NFTs and buildings are never taken.
  3. You win only with twice the defense. Then every defender falls and you can loot; otherwise both sides lose troops and nothing is taken.
  4. New villages get a 7-day shield. They cannot attack or be attacked.
  5. A village can be raided successfully at most 3 times a day. What sits in its Vault is safe.
  6. Pick on someone your size. Raids on villages 3 or more Town Hall levels below yours give half loot and no ranking points.
  7. No trading between players. The Market only exchanges your own resources, and SEED cannot be exchanged.

Fair play

  1. No feeder accounts. Don't run extra wallets just to be raided by, or to feed, a main village.
  2. No bug abuse. If something gives you resources or wins it shouldn't, report it instead of using it.
  3. Keep names clean. No offensive, hateful or impersonating player or clan names.
  4. Leave the site alone. Don't attack or overload the website, the contracts' readers or other players' devices.
  5. Report, don't retaliate. Use the report form below for bugs and rule-breakers.

FAQ

Where does it run?

Season 1 runs on the Sepolia testnet, next to Squeekchain's own contracts.

Do I need a Squeek?

No. Every wallet plays one village with the same production and SEED. Squeeks add one champion and faster building; more Squeeks help less and less.

Testnet or mainnet?

The game runs on the Sepolia testnet now as a practice season. Mainnet Season 1 opens after the official Squeek mint, and testnet progress does not carry over.

Can someone take my Squeek?

No. Raids only take Wood, Food, Stone and Iron. SEED, NFTs and buildings are never taken.

Do I need to be online to defend?

No. Battles resolve on arrival with the troops and Walls you have at home.

Can I trade with other players?

No player trading. The Market only exchanges your own resources, which keeps multi-accounts from feeding a main village.
