# Art prompts (Artlist)

All art was generated in the Artlist web app with **Seedream 5.0 Pro** at 2K, using three text-only style kits (no reference images): *SqueekNest Köy · Harita* (map), *Binalar* (buildings), *Karakterler* (characters). Sprites are generated on flat magenta and cut out with a chroma key into `art/sprites/`.

## 1 · Village map (ring layout) · kit: Map · 16:9

```
Top-down 3/4 view game map in high-detail 16-bit pixel art, warm afternoon light. A round hamster village in the exact center, enclosed by a low wooden palisade with pointed stakes and four small wooden gates on the diagonals. Inside the palisade: a small round cobblestone plaza in the middle with a small round town hall with cream walls, a blue conical slate roof, a wooden wheel emblem and a red flag; four cobblestone roads running diagonally from the plaza to the four gates; a thin dirt lane looping around the plaza; twelve round empty dirt building plots with stone rims in two rings, eight outer and four inner, three of them with small cozy cottages and one with a striped market stall. A clear blue river flows outside the palisade and circles the whole village in a slightly irregular ring with sandy banks. Four river branches flow from the ring straight out to the top, bottom, left and right edges of the map and split the countryside into four separate quarters. A small wooden plank bridge crosses the river at each diagonal gate, and a dirt road continues into each quarter and forks to two production sites. Top-left quarter: two dense forest groves with a log pile. Top-right quarter: two fenced sunflower fields. Bottom-left quarter: two terraced grey stone quarry pits with a wooden crane and cut stone blocks. Bottom-right quarter: two rocky hills with rust-orange iron ore and timber-framed mine entrances with minecarts. The village circle fills about 45% of the image width, buildings are small, tiny hamster villagers walk the roads, scattered trees along the map border. Crisp pixels, no anti-aliasing, no text, no UI.
```
